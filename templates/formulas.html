Mechanics O-Level / A-Level

Kinematics Equations (SUVAT)

$$v = u + at$$ $$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$$ $$v^2 = u^2 + 2as$$ $$s = \frac{(u + v)}{2}t$$

Fundamental equations for linear motion with constant acceleration $a$, initial speed $u$, final speed $v$, time $t$, and displacement $s$.

Mechanics All Levels

Newton's Laws & Momentum

$$\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = m\vec{a} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$$ $$\vec{p} = m\vec{v}$$ $$J = \Delta \vec{p} = \int \vec{F} \, dt$$

Rate of change of linear momentum equals resultant external force. In closed systems, total momentum remains conserved: $\sum p_i = \sum p_f$.

Mechanics / Astro O/A-Level

Universal Gravitation & Orbit

$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$ $$g = \frac{GM}{r^2}, \quad v_{\text{orbit}} = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$$ $$T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM}r^3$$

Newton's inverse-square gravitational law and Kepler's 3rd Law of planetary orbital periods where $G \approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.

Mechanics All Levels

Work-Energy Theorem & Power

$$W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{d} = F d \cos\theta$$ $$E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2, \quad E_p = mgh$$ $$P = \frac{dW}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$$

Work done on an object equals the net change in kinetic energy: $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta E_k$. Mechanical energy is conserved without non-conservative forces.

Calculus A-Level Pure

Differential Calculus Rules

$$\frac{d}{dx}[x^n] = n x^{n-1}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}[uv] = u'v + uv'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{u}{v}\right] = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$$

Power, product, quotient, and chain rules for evaluating slopes, tangent lines, and instantaneous rates of change.

Calculus A-Level Pure

Integral Calculus & Parts

$$\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \quad (n \neq -1)$$ $$\int \frac{1}{x} \, dx = \ln|x| + C$$ $$\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du$$ $$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

Antiderivatives, integration by parts, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus linking area under curves with antiderivatives.

Electromagnetism O/A-Level

Electrostatics & Capacitance

$$F_e = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$$ $$E = \frac{F}{q} = \frac{V}{d}, \quad V = \frac{k q}{r}$$ $$Q = C V, \quad U = \frac{1}{2} C V^2$$

Coulomb's Law, electric field intensity, electrostatic potential, and energy stored in dielectric parallel-plate capacitor plates.

Electromagnetism All Levels

Ohm's & Kirchhoff's Circuit Laws

$$V = I R, \quad P = I V = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$$ $$R_{\text{series}} = R_1 + R_2 + \dots$$ $$\frac{1}{R_{\text{parallel}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \dots$$ $$\sum I_{\text{in}} = \sum I_{\text{out}}, \quad \sum \Delta V = 0$$

Ohm's relationship for resistive elements, Joule heating dissipation, and Kirchhoff's Current & Voltage conservation laws.

Waves & Optics All Levels

Wave Velocity & Snell's Law

$$v = f \lambda, \quad T = \frac{1}{f}$$ $$n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2$$ $$\sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1} \quad (n_1 > n_2)$$ $$d \sin\theta = m \lambda \quad (\text{Diffraction})$$

Wave propagation velocity, boundary refraction indices, total internal reflection condition, and Young's double-slit interference.

Thermodynamics A-Level

Ideal Gas & First Law

$$P V = n R T = N k_B T$$ $$\Delta U = Q - W$$ $$Q = m c \Delta T, \quad Q = m L$$ $$\bar{E}_k = \frac{3}{2} k_B T$$

Macroscopic equation of state for dilute gases, internal energy changes, specific heat capacities, and microscopic kinetic temperature theory.

Algebra O-Level

Quadratic Formula & Discriminant

$$a x^2 + b x + c = 0$$ $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$ $$\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$$ $$x_{\text{vertex}} = -\frac{b}{2a}$$

Roots of second-degree equations: $\Delta > 0$ yields 2 real roots, $\Delta = 0$ gives 1 repeated root, and $\Delta < 0$ produces conjugate complex roots.

Trig & Vectors O/A-Level

Trigonometric & Vector Laws

$$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$$ $$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = |\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\cos\theta = A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z$$ $$|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = |\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\sin\theta$$ $$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos(C)$$

Fundamental Pythagorean trig identity, dot products (scalar projection, work), cross products (torque, magnetic force), and Law of Cosines.
